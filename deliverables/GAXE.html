GenAI Assistant Deliverable

Terminal 84B, my GenAI Assistant. It was made with Gemini 3, and is emulating a legacy mainframe computer

Terminal 84B's response to a prompt

T84B is meant to be a grumpy, legacy system that resents the current tech landscape for being bloated and unnecessary. I thought picking a legacy system for my GenAI assistant's character was very meta and humorous, and fits very well with the overall aesthetic of my website.

I plan on either linking T84B on my homepage, or integrating its services elsewhere on the website.

The 'character' was implemented was through the system prompt, as a directive of sorts. I used Gemini 3 to help me find a good balance of grumpiness and helpfulness, and help me generate the ASCII boilerplate that helps with T84B's style and aesthetic.

Reflection on GenAI

I still cannot really believe that all ChatGPT and other LLMs do is fancy-word-guessing. It feels wrong and very monkey-typewriter to me. I don't buy into that thought experiment, the same way I don't buy into the supernatural properties of π (the whole thing where it contains everything in the universe).

One quote that stood out in particular was from thebullshitmachines.com, where they say: "As anthropoglossic systems, LLMs tap into our evolved heuristic—effective for tens of thousands of years—that if something can talk, it must be able to think."
